# Privacy Policy for The Millers Hearth

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026  
**Provider:** Millerprise Entertainment  

The Millers Hearth ("the Channel") is committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our Roku channel.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
We do not require user accounts, logins, names, email addresses, or personal registration to watch The Millers Hearth. We do not directly collect or store personally identifiable information from viewers.

## 2. Advertising & Roku Platform Data
The Millers Hearth is a free, ad-supported channel distributed through the Roku Streaming Store. Roku and its authorized advertising partners may collect device identifiers (such as Roku Advertising Identifier / RIDA), IP addresses, and viewing metrics in accordance with Roku's Privacy Policy to deliver advertisements and measure channel performance. You can manage your advertising preferences directly in your Roku device's Privacy settings.

## 3. Children's Privacy
The Millers Hearth is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
